# Known limitations

Deliberate boundaries of this release, and items that need a decision from Veritek before they are switched on.

## By design (from the specification)

- **Not an accounting or ERP system.** Invoices and receipts mirror the accounting system; the register
  shows a *data as of* date and reconciliation status. There is no ledger, GST return or e-invoicing.
- **Broad production phases only** (received → production → QC → ready → dispatched). No routings, work
  orders, bills of material or inventory reservation. Stock quantity on a product is informational.
- **No automatic sending.** The CRM records that a quotation, update or dispatch notice was sent; it does not
  send email or WhatsApp itself. Phone, WhatsApp and email links open the user's own app.
- **Money is never converted between currencies.** Totals, targets and charts are per currency; charts plot
  the reporting currency (INR) and say so. Catalogue prices apply only to quotations in the price-list currency.
- **Order value is not revenue or margin**, and contacts logged are activity, not sales output — reports say so.

## Current limitations

- **Single server, SQLite database.** Suits Veritek's team size (tens of users). It needs one host with a
  persistent disk; it is not a horizontally scaled multi-server setup. The serverless (Vercel) deployment is a
  demo whose data resets on cold start.
- **Quotation documents are generated by the browser's print dialog** (Print / PDF on the quotation). There
  is no server-side PDF engine or custom template designer; the issued PDF can be attached when recording
  the issue.
- **Pipeline stages are changed on the enquiry page**, not by dragging cards, because every stage change asks
  for a reason or next action.
- **Single sign-in method:** email + password with optional authenticator-app MFA. No SSO (Google/Microsoft) yet.
- **Notifications are in-app only.** No email or push delivery of alerts and daily summaries.
- **Imports** cover customers, enquiries and open order lines (plus objects). Products, invoices and
  historical quotations are entered in the app.
- **Retention and permanent deletion** are not enabled: records are archived or deactivated. Define a
  retention policy with Veritek before enabling deletion (spec section 36).
- **Offline use** is not supported; a connection is needed (failed saves keep the entered data for a retry).
