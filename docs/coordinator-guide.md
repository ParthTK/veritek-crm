# Coordinator quick guide

For the person who keeps the CRM current for everyone else.

## Start of the day

- **Dashboard**: enquiries not replied, pending quotations, delayed orders and follow-ups due.
- **Follow-ups**: everyone's overdue and today's next actions. Pick one, record what happened, set the next
  date, Save. *Reschedule*, *Reassign* and *Cancel* are under the form.
- The search box (Ctrl+K) finds a company by name, phone, email, enquiry, order or PO number.

## Customers come first

**Customers → New customer** asks for the company, a contact, phone (with its country code) or email, city,
country, state, how they found us and who it is assigned to. If it looks like an existing customer, you are
shown the match before anything is created. Everything else goes on the customer's page later.

## Enquiries

- **New enquiry** (yellow button, top bar): customer, product (or describe what they need), quantity if known,
  source, who it is assigned to, and when to follow up. If the source is Exhibition, choose which one.
- **Enquiries → Board**: drag a card to another column to change its status. While you drag, **Won**, **Lost**
  and **Cancelled** appear at the bottom. If the move needs something (a next action, a review date, a reason),
  a short form opens first. On a phone, open the enquiry and use *Change status*.
- An enquiry shows *Not replied* until the first call, email or message is recorded.

## Quotations

1. On the enquiry, **New quotation**: products, quantities and prices come from the enquiry and the product
   list; adjust them, check the terms, **Save quotation**.
2. **Mark as sent** when it has gone to the customer: how it was sent, valid until, and when to follow up.
3. When the customer decides: **Approved** or **Rejected** (with the reason). To change the offer, make a
   **New version**.
4. **Create order** on an approved quotation: quantities, prices, PO number and the dispatch date for each item
   (leave the date blank if it is not agreed yet).

A repeat order without a quotation: **Orders → New order**.

## Orders

- **Record payment**: the amount, date and UTR or cheque number. The order shows its total, what has come in and
  the balance; the status moves from *Advance pending* to *Advance received*, *Part paid* and *Paid*.
- **Update progress** on each item: stage, expected dispatch date, quantity ready, and a delay with its reason.
  Only Quality can pass an item from QC to ready. **Production** shows every item by stage with the same form.
- If an item will go later than promised, the sales person gets a follow-up to tell the customer;
  **Tell the customer** records what they said.
- **Record dispatch**: quantities, transporter, LR or tracking number, vehicle. Mark **Delivered** when it
  arrives. A wrong dispatch is **reversed** with a reason, never deleted.
- **Cancel quantity** only takes off what is still to be sent; what has gone stays on record.

## Sales pipeline

Every live deal at one of six steps: enquiry, quotation, confirmation, payment, production, dispatch. Red and
amber cards are stuck, and the card says why.

Drag a card on to the next column to take that step; a short form opens first:

| Drop on | What it does |
|---|---|
| Quotation | Starts the quotation from the enquiry's products |
| Confirmation | Records that the customer approved the sent quotation |
| Payment or Production | Creates the order from the approved quotation (or confirms a draft order) |
| Production (from Payment) | Records the advance |
| Dispatch | Marks what is ready |
| **Dispatched** (appears at the bottom while you drag an order) | Records the dispatch |
| **Lost** (appears while you drag an enquiry) | Closes the enquiry as lost, with the reason |

A card does not go back or skip a step: the reason shows and the card stays. The card then sits wherever its
records put it. On a phone, open the card and use its next-step button.

## Exhibitions

Add each exhibition, then **Add visitor** for every customer you meet (add new companies under Customers first).
**New enquiry** on a visitor starts an enquiry already linked to that exhibition.

## Golden rules

1. Record what actually happened, and when. Never guess dates or quantities; leave them blank.
2. A plan is not a dispatch, a promise is not a payment, approved is not won until there is an order.
3. Never re-type history to correct it: use reverse, undo, or change with a reason.
