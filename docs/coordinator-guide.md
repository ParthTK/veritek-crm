# Coordinator quick guide

For the data coordinator — the person who keeps the CRM current for everyone else.

## Start of the day: Work queue

**Work queue** (left menu) collects what needs chasing today:

| Tab | What to do |
|---|---|
| **New inputs** | New enquiries without a first response; draft orders with missing details; technical issues mentioned in calls → *Register complaint* |
| **Follow-ups due** | Overdue and today's tasks for everyone; record the outcome or nudge the owner |
| **Orders needing updates** | Lines with no fresh production information, overdue or at-risk lines, customers not yet told of a date change, ready lines not dispatched |
| **Missing information** | Products or quantities still unknown, commitments not agreed |

The search box (Ctrl+K) finds a company by name, phone, email, enquiry, order or customer PO.

## Quick entry (yellow button, top bar)

The fastest way to record what someone tells you:

- **New enquiry** — customer (search, or add with just a phone *or* email), product (or “unspecified” with a
  short requirement), quantity if known, source (if the source is Exhibition, choose which exhibition — it is required), owner, next action.
- **Follow-up** — what happened on a call or visit, and the next action in the same step.
- **Order update** — phase and status of a line, delay reason, expected ready and dispatch dates.
- **Dispatch** — what actually left today, or a plan for a future date.

## Recording on someone else's behalf

Most updates come from people who do not sign in. In every update form:

- **Reported by** — choose the person who gave you the information (required for production updates in
  coordinator mode), or type a name if they are not in the list.
- **When it happened / as of** — leave as now, or enter the earlier time; it is kept as a back-entry and both
  times stay visible.

## Orders

- **Production board** shows every open line by phase, most urgent first. *Update* opens the quick form.
  - *No change — still on track* confirms the line without inventing new information.
  - A forecast later than the original promise creates a task for the sales owner to tell the customer —
    the customer has **not** agreed until a conversation is recorded (*Record customer update* on the order).
  - Moving a line back (e.g. QC → production for rework) needs a short note.
  - Only Quality (or you, naming Quality as the reporter) can pass a line from QC to ready.
- **Order page → Edit order details**: factory, production and dispatch owners, priority, PO reference,
  addresses; commercial terms (payment terms, advance, freight) need the coordinator, commercial or management.
- **Cancel quantity** only for the remaining balance; dispatched quantity is never erased.

## Dispatches

- **Plan a dispatch** for a future date — it changes no balance.
- **Post as dispatched** when the goods have actually left (adjust quantities to what really went). Sending
  quantity not recorded as ready needs a reason.
- Add transport details (carrier, LR/AWB, vehicle, e-way bill, tracking link) any time.
- **Delivered** — date, who received it, signed POD.
- **Customer informed** — only when the customer was actually told.
- A wrong dispatch is **reversed** with a reason (management), never deleted.

## Quotations and conversion

- Drafts that need approval show *Needs approval*; the owner sends them for approval, and an approver
  decides on Team & approvals.
- *Record as sent* needs the sent time, how it was sent, the PDF or a summary, validity and the next follow-up.
- When the PO arrives: open the quotation → **Convert to order**, enter the accepted quantities (less than
  quoted is fine), PO reference and committed dispatch dates (or *Not agreed yet*). A double click cannot
  create two orders.

## Sales pipeline

**Sales pipeline** shows every live deal at one of six steps: enquiry, quotation, confirmation, payment,
production, dispatch. Red and amber cards are stuck, and the card says why. Open a card for the full journey and
links to its enquiry, quotation and order. Nothing is entered here: a deal moves when the underlying record changes.

## Exhibitions

Add each exhibition, then record the **customers visited** (add the customer first if they are new). **Raise
enquiry** on a visitor starts an enquiry already linked to the exhibition.

## Customers

- Before adding a company the system shows possible existing matches — use the existing one when it is the
  same company; ask an administrator to **merge** duplicates (nothing is lost).
- Keep the state filled in: it sets the sales region and therefore who sees and approves the customer.

## Golden rules

1. Record what actually happened, with who said it and when — never guess dates or quantities; leave them unknown.
2. A plan is not a dispatch, a promise is not a payment, accepted is not won.
3. Never re-type history to correct it: use the correction actions (reverse, amend with reason).
